 Keyboard Skills and Techniques
These are a set of skills required to operate a
     keyboard smoothly while typing.

--- page 7 ---
   Hunt and Peck Style
  (two-fingered typing)

The typist presses each key
       individually.
 Disadvantage: The few
 fingers that are used are
  forced to move a much
greater distance within the
         keyboard.

--- page 8 ---
       Buffering Style


It allows the typist to look at
the source copy, memorizes
 one or more sentences, and
 then looks at the keyboard
    and type the sentences.

--- page 9 ---
      Thumbing Style


It can be accomplished using
 one or both thumbs to enter
characters on the device like
    tablets and cellphones.

--- page 10 ---
        Hybrid Style


This style combines different
 kinds of typing processes
 that can be useful to their
            work.

--- page 11 ---
        Touch Style


This refers to typing without
  looking to find keys. The
typist knows the character in
        the keyboard.
