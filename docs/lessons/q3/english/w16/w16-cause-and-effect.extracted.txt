INTRODUCTION
 Cause and effect are important elements of a text
 that help the reader to follow a writer’s line of
 thought, regardless of whether that text is fiction or
 nonfiction.

 Writing a cause-and-effect paragraph involves
 understanding the logic or connection between
 cause and effect. It requires a knowledge of
 the expressions and vocabulary used to express
 causes and effects. Cause-effect transitions are
 important components of writing.


========== page 04 (text) ==========
        CAUSE
Cause is the reason why something
 happens. It is the producer of the
effect. It answers the question “why
     something happened?”


========== page 05 (text) ==========
EFFECT
It is the results, outcomes, result,
conditions or occurrences
produced by the cause. It answers
the question “what happened?”
