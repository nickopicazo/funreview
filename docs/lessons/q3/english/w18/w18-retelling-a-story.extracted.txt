WHAT IS MYTH
• A myth is a colorful story based upon tradition or
  legend that tries to explain natural phenomena or
  why things are the way they are.
• Usually features Greek gods and goddesses who
  interact with ordinary human beings.
• The god and goddesses have human feelings.
• Focuses on good and evil.
• often has magic in it
• teaches some kind of lesson.

SNOW
WHITE
Once upon a time, there was a beautiful princess named Snow
White. Her stepmother, the Queen, cared about her looks. The
Queen had a Magic Mirror and always asked, "Mirror, mirror on the
wall, who is the fairest of them all?"


One day, the mirror said Snow White was the fairest!
The jealous Queen told a hunter to take Snow White into the forest
and never bring her back.
