========== page 09 (text) ==========
      Interrogative Pronouns
Examples:

 Pronoun    Used to Ask About        Example Sentence

   Who           a person            Who is your teacher?

  Whom       a person (formal)   Whom did you see at school?

  Whose        possession             Whose bag is this?

  Which          a choice          Which pencil do you like?

  What            a thing        What is your favorite subject?

  1. Who is standing near the flagpole?
  2. What is inside the box?

  4. Whose pencil is on the floor?
  5. To whom are you speaking?


========== page 12 (text) ==========
  Q3 W13 Practice: Our School Garden
Our class planted vegetables in the garden. Some pupils
 watered the plants, while others pulled out the weeds.
     The teacher said, “Who wants to pick the ripe
        tomatoes?”
   Questions:
                   Everyone   raised their hands!

  1. What interrogative pronoun is
     used in the story?
  2. What question did the teacher
     ask?
  3. How did the pupils respond?


========== page 13 (text) ==========
                Ask and Answer
                  Pair up the pupils.
Each pair takes turns asking and answering questions
            using interrogative pronouns.
Sample questions:


 1. Who is your class adviser?
 2. What subject do you like most?
 3. Which color do you prefer, red or
    blue?
 4. Whose notebook is this?
