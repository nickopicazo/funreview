========== page 28 (text) ==========
REMEMBER!
 Possessive pronouns are pronouns
 used to show ownership or
 possession.
 Possessive pronouns that take the
 place of nouns are my, your, his,
 her, its, our and their.


========== page 29 (text) ==========
REMEMBER!
 Possessive pronouns that may be
 used without nouns are mine, yours,
 his, hers, ours, and theirs.
 NOTE: possessive pronouns do not
 use the apostrophe.
