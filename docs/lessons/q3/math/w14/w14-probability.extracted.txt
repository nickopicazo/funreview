========== page 05 (text) ==========
🌈 Four Probability Words


   ✓   CERTAIN      It will happen.



  👍    LIKELY       It will probably happen.



  🤔    UNLIKELY     It probably will not happen.



   ✖   IMPOSSIBLE   It cannot happen.

========== page 11 (text) ==========
🕵 Activity 2: Probability Detectives

 Work with your group. Read each situation and decide the probability word.


   1    A jar has 10 green beads and no purple beads. Pick a purple bead.



   2    There are 9 sunny-day cards and 1 rainy-day card. Pick a sunny card.



   3    A normal school day has classes scheduled.




                  Be ready to explain: “I chose _____ because _____.”

                Include a picture and one sentence explaining each prediction.
