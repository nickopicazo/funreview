GUIDED EXAMPLES


Zero Pattern Progression




               TENS (1 ZERO)         HUNDREDS (2 ZEROS)        THOUSANDS (3 ZEROS)


                  70 × 8                 500 × 9                  2,000 × 4
             7 × 8 = 56 (+ 1 zero)    5 × 9 = 45 (+ 2 zeros)     2 × 4 = 8 (+ 3 zeros)


                  = 560                 = 4,500                    = 8,000
